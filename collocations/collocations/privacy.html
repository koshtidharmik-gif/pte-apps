Privacy policy

PTE Collocations Coach · Last updated: 29 September 2026

PTE Collocations Coach ("the app") is an independent study app for learning the collocations in Pearson's Academic Collocation List. It is not made by or connected to Pearson. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details you give us: first name, last name, email address, country code and mobile number, and your password (stored securely by Google Firebase; we never see it).
  • Profile picture, if you choose to add one.
  • Learning data: your answers in the games, mistakes, progress, and daily and monthly results, used to show your reports and explain your mistakes.
  • Purchase status: whether you have bought lifetime full access. Payments are handled by Stripe or Google Play; we never receive or store your card details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app shows no ads.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in on any device.
  • To save your progress and show your performance reports and mistakes.
  • To give access to features you have paid for.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as email verification and password reset.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which keeps it encrypted in transit and at rest. Only you can read your own data from the app. Text-to-speech uses the voice built into your device.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Detailed answer history is kept for about 60 days. When you delete your account, your account and all its data are deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: tap your profile picture, then Delete my account. You can also ask us to delete it by email; see how to delete your account.

Children

The app is meant for people preparing for the PTE test and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
